## Get the app

To check **your own** home network, use the netcheck app on a computer connected to that network:

1. Install **nmap** once (free): <https://nmap.org/download> (Mac: the `.dmg`, Windows: the `.exe`, keep the defaults).
2. Download **netcheck** for your computer and double-click it:
   * Mac: `netcheck.app` — the first time, right-click it → **Open** (the app is not signed yet).
   * Windows: `netcheck.exe` — if Windows warns, click **More info → Run anyway**.
3. Your browser opens. Tick *"this is my network"* and press **Quick check**.
4. Press **Show QR code** to see the results on your phone (same Wi-Fi, read-only, stops after 30 minutes).

netcheck only checks; it never changes anything. Everything stays on your computer.
